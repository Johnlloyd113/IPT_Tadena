21. What the system does
A camera watches the library seats and checks whether each seat is taken or vacant. The program looks at the camera view and decides the seat status. Library staff can see the results on a dashboard. This helps people know which seats are available.

22. What we store
We only store the following information:
- Time of the seat-status record
- Seat name or seat number
- Seat status: Taken or Vacant

23. What we do NOT store
We do not store photos, videos, faces, or names. The system does not identify individual library users.

24. Who can see it
Only library staff with valid login credentials can view the dashboard and stored records. Access is limited to the information needed to monitor seat availability.

25. Where the camera points
The camera will point only at the seats in the approved area. It will not be aimed at faces or computer or mobile screens. A small notice will be placed in the area to inform library users about the research and the use of the camera.
